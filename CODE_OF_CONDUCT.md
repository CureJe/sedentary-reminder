# Community code of conduct

Everyone is welcome to participate in Stand Up Buddy. Discuss the work with
respect, including when disagreeing about a design, reviewing a contribution,
or reporting a bug. Be patient with different experience levels and communicate
in a way that lets others reproduce and understand the problem.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate
disruption, and publishing another person's private information are not welcome.
Critique ideas and behavior without personal attacks.

These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests, and other
project-managed discussions. They also apply when someone explicitly represents
the project in another space.

## Reporting and moderation

Use GitHub's **Report content** option for abusive content. For a non-sensitive
project moderation concern, open an issue and link the relevant discussion.
Do not post personal information or confidential allegations publicly; request
a private contact channel without details if one is needed. This project does
not currently operate a dedicated confidential moderation inbox.

The maintainer, [CureJe](https://github.com/CureJe), may ask for changes, remove
content, lock a discussion, or restrict participation in response to violations.
Responses should take severity and context into account. Reports and relevant
context will be considered; response times cannot be guaranteed.
